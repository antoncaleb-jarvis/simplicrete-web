Good To Know

Common questions

What is microcement — is it just painted concrete?

No. It's a cement-based system trowelled on in layers and sealed. It bonds to the surface, wears like concrete, and gives a continuous finish with no joints or grout lines. Paint sits on top and chips; microcement is part of the surface.

Can you go over my existing tiles or floor?

Usually yes. Microcement and self-levelling overlays are designed to go over sound tiles, screed or concrete once the surface is properly prepared. We confirm at the assessment.

How thick is it — will it raise my floor height?

Microcement adds only about 2 to 3 mm, so doors and skirtings are rarely affected. Overlays and stone carpet are thicker; we'll tell you the build-up for your job.

How long does it take, and when can I use it?

Depends on the area and the system. A bathroom might be a few days; a large floor longer. Light use typically after a few days, full cure and heavy traffic a little later. Your quote spells out the timeline.

Does it work outdoors?

Yes — there are specific exterior systems for patios, pool surrounds and driveways, with UV-stable sealers. We won't put an indoor-only product outside.

Will it crack?

These systems flex more than tile and we treat existing cracks and movement joints during prep. No finish is bulletproof, but done right on a sound substrate it holds up well.

What colours can I get?

A wide pigment range across matte to satin. We bring samples to the assessment so you can see them in your own light.

What drives the price?

Area, the state of the existing surface, how much prep and levelling is needed, the system chosen, and access. The assessment turns that into a fixed number.
